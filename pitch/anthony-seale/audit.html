Five findings, five fixes

None of these are effort problems. They’re ownership and plumbing problems — all fixable.

1You’re building on rented land

sealeteam865.com points at a brokerage-provided Delta Media template branded for Ferguson Realtors. Your domain equity, content, and rankings are tied to a platform you don’t control.

A personal site you own — your domain, your hosting, your brand — with listings pulled in via IDX so nothing is lost.

2Zero visible social proof

“Not yet rated (0 reviews)” is what buyers see, from an agent who’s been a top producer since 2016. The single highest-converting element on a realtor site is missing entirely — and you’ve already earned it.

Automated review collection after every closing: one-tap links for past and future clients, displayed on your site and Google.

3501 posts with nowhere to land

500+ posts for 309 followers means the content is running without a funnel. Attention with no landing pages, no lead capture, and no retargeting is effort that evaporates.

Landing pages per niche (cabins, land, lakefront, investment) so every post links somewhere that captures the contact.

4Your leads aren’t yours

Brokerage IDX templates route inquiries into the brokerage’s system. You’re an investor — you know the difference between owning the asset and renting access to it. Right now the pipeline is rented.

Forms and calls route directly to your CRM and your phone. You own the list, forever.

5Your real edge is invisible

Cabins, marinas, land, investment property — and you own rentals in three counties yourself. That’s a genuinely rare niche in East Tennessee, and a template site flattens it into “generic agent.”

A personal brand built around the specialist story: the agent who invests where you’re buying.
